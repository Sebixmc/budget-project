How to export your transactions

Capital One (Savor Card, Checking, Savings)

  1. Log in at capitalone.com
  2. Click on the account you want to export
  3. Click the Download icon near the transaction list
  4. Select CSV as the format and choose a date range
  5. Save and upload the file above

UCCU (Utah Community Credit Union)

  1. Log in at uccu.com → Online Banking
  2. Select the account you want to export
  3. Click Account History or the download/export option
  4. Choose CSV format and your date range
  5. Save the file (usually named AccountHistory.csv) and upload it above
